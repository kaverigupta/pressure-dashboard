How pressure is defined

Chase: win-probability between 25% and 75% after the powerplay, or 5+ wickets down before over 17 with RRR 8+.
First innings: death overs (17-20) or a collapse (3+ down in overs 1-6, 5+ by over 14, 7+ by over 18).
Score = 0.60 x shrunk pressure value + 0.25 x hit-rate + 0.15 x delta vs own ordinary baseline. Value = runs above expected, minus 10 runs per extra dismissal (bowlers: runs saved plus wicket credit).
